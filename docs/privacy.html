Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

VibeFlow is a voice keyboard that turns your speech into clean, formatted text. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have — in plain language.

The short version.
  • We don’t sell your data and we show no ads.
  • We don’t store your voice recordings. Speech is turned into text on your device by default; if you turn off “On-device only”, audio is processed by Apple’s speech service. Either way, VibeFlow never receives or keeps your audio.
  • Your dictated text is sent to our AI provider only to format it, then it’s gone — we don’t keep it.
  • The keyboard can’t see passwords or other secure fields, and doesn’t log your keystrokes.
  • You can delete your account and data anytime in Settings → Delete account.

2. Who processes your data (service providers)

We use a small set of trusted providers to run VibeFlow. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — accounts, authentication and app settings (our backend).
  • OpenAI — formats your dictated text; the text is processed to produce your result and is not used to train models on our behalf or retained by us.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and purchase validation.
  • Apple — on iOS, converts your speech to text (on-device by default; via Apple’s cloud speech service if you turn off “On-device only”). On Android the device’s own speech recognizer does this.
  • Google Play / Apple — process payments and subscriptions.

We don’t sell your personal data or share it with third parties for their own advertising.

3. Tracking & advertising

VibeFlow contains no advertising SDKs and no cross-app tracking. We do not use the advertising identifier, and on iOS we don’t show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because we don’t track you.

4. How your information is protected

All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS between the app, our backend, and our providers. Access to backend data is restricted to authorized systems.

5. Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account and data — in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, usage history and device list. (An anonymous per-device usage counter, not linked to your identity, may be retained to prevent abuse.)
  • Cancel your subscription — manage or cancel anytime in your Google Play or Apple subscriptions. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a store subscription.
  • Access or questions — contact us at the address below and we’ll help.

Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. You can exercise these by contacting us.

6. Children

VibeFlow is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. Dictated text and audio are processed transiently and not retained. When you delete your account, associated personal data is removed promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as VibeFlow evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.
