## Full description (max 4000)
VibeFlow turns your voice into clean, well-formatted text — instantly, and
privately.

Just tap and talk. VibeFlow transcribes what you say with high accuracy, removes
the "ums" and "uhs" automatically, and hands you tidy text you can use anywhere —
messages, emails, notes, docs.

WHY VIBEFLOW
• Fast & accurate — modern on-device speech recognition that keeps up with how
  you actually talk.
• Private by design — your dictation is processed on your device. Your words are
  yours.
• Clean output — filler words and stumbles are cleaned up automatically, so you
  don't have to.
• Smart formatting (optional) — turn a rambling thought into a polished message
  when you want it, while keeping your exact meaning.
• Works the way you speak — natural, continuous dictation, no robotic pauses.

GREAT FOR
• Firing off messages and emails hands-free
• Capturing notes and ideas the moment they strike
• Anyone who thinks faster than they type

VibeFlow is also available on iPhone, Windows, and Mac — the same premium
voice-to-text, everywhere you work.
